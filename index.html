House Lights

Each house connects with its own LIFX token, which stays on this phone only. Houses never affect each other.

  1. Open cloud.lifx.com/settings and sign in with that house’s LIFX account.
  2. Tap Generate New Token, name it “House Lights”, and copy it.
  3. Name the house and paste the token below.
