House Lights

Control the LIFX lights on your house. Your access token stays on this phone only.

  1. On this phone, open cloud.lifx.com/settings and sign in with your LIFX account.
  2. Tap Generate New Token, name it “House Lights”, and copy it.
  3. Paste it below.
